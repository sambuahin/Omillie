I have fought the good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith: Henceforth there is laid up for me a crown of righteousness with the Lord, the righteous Judge shall give me that day; And not to me only, but unto all them also that love His appearing. - 2 Tim 4:7-8 KJV

Sis. Millicent Buahin joined the church at Mr. King’s residence and was among the pioneering Ga-Dangme Methodists who faithfully attended the evening services held there. She played a significant role in the early growth and establishment of the church community.

She was instrumental in the formation of the Ebenezer Methodist Society Women’s Fellowship, serving faithfully as a Branch Treasurer before rising to the position of President from 1991 to 1993, and later as Circuit Treasurer. Her leadership, dedication, and commitment helped shape the fellowship into a strong and vibrant organization during her tenure.

Sis. Millicent was a devoted foundational member of the Ebenezer Methodist Church Choir, together with her children. She was also a founding member of the Singing Band and actively participated in its formation. A gifted alto singer, her voice added richness to the worship life of the church. In her generosity and love for the choir, she personally purchased the white choir stoles for the choristers. In recognition of her dedication and motherly guidance, she was later appointed Choir Mother.

She was a committed member of Sister Grace B. Larnyoh’s Class, now known as the Jeremiah Class. Because of her exemplary conduct, faithfulness, and commitment to duty, she was appointed an Assistant Class Leader, serving diligently and nurturing the spiritual growth of members with love, discipline, and humility.

Due to relocation and ill health, Sis. Millicent could no longer attend church regularly and was consequently listed among the infirm members. Despite this, she remained dear to the church and was remembered with love and prayers. During this period, the Reverend Ministers and Stewards visited her monthly to fellowship with her and administer Holy Communion.

The church celebrates the life of Sis. Millicent Buahin with gratitude to God for her faithful service, godly example, and lasting legacy. May her soul rest in perfect peace, and may her life continue to inspire generations to serve God faithfully.
