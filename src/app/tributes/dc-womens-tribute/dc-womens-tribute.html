Dansoman Circuit Women's Fellowship Tribute

Tis Jesus the first and the last Whose spirit shall guide us safe home We’ll praise Him for all that is past And trust Him for all that’s to come - MHB 69 V2

Sis. Millicent Buahin was inducted into office as the Circuit Treasurer for the period 2002 to 2005.

She served alongside the late Sis. Lydia Annan as President and Sis. Comfort Darku as Secretary. It was during their tenure that a Girls’ Fellowship member from the Dansoman Circuit was sponsored to the UK.

She was a member of the Ebenezer Women’s Fellowship. Aunty Millie, as she was affectionately called by all, was dedicated and committed to the cause of the fellowship. As Circuit Treasurer, she performed her duties diligently and managed the finances of the circuit with excellence. During their tenure as officers of the Circuit Fellowship, the Dansoman Circuit discharged its financial obligations honourably, to the admiration of all.

Sis. Millicent was always punctual at circuit meetings and would never miss a conference of the Methodist Women’s Fellowship. It was always a joy to see her happily preparing for an upcoming conference. She would always carry food and food items, and she delighted in preparing meals for anyone—especially if you were from the Dansoman or Accra Circuit. Her small stove and cooking utensils always accompanied her.

Sis. Millicent was very friendly, kind, humble, and easily approachable. She loved to sing and always had a story to share after late-night programmes at conferences. She made everyone around her laugh with her songs, and she had a special way of getting people to dance along.

Sis. Millicent, you will be sorely missed by all your sisters in the circuit.

May your gentle soul rest in perfect peace.

Alagalugu Alugulaga.

Onukpa Millie, yaawo jogbann.
