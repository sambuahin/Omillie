Children's Tribute

“For if we live, we live to the Lord; and if we die, we die to the Lord. Therefore, whether we live or die, we are the Lord’s.” - Romans 14:7–8

Omillie, our mother, friend, and confidant. How can we stand here and express to the world the kind of mother we had? How do we begin?

Shall we speak of you being our voice of reasoning, our strong tower, our greatest source of encouragement, or the magnet that brought us all together in love and peace?

No… this is difficult for us to accept that you are no more. As we navigate this new reality, we appreciate you even more, and it is our deepest wish that you could have stayed a little longer. To the world, you were a woman. To your colleagues, you were a mother. To us, your children, you are our hero. You planted God’s Word in us and guided us to live lives filled with righteousness and grace. These qualities are what we hold on to.

Omillie, you were gentle, kind, and selfless, and you served in the house of the Lord with all your strength and might. This assures us of your place in heaven, resting in peace and love.

We are all experiencing your absence, Ameley. The kitchen is quiet, and we no longer smell your special everyday aroma. Omillie, our father is missing you deeply, and he is so lost without your presence in the house. This is new to him continuing life without you. Where are you going, leaving us all behind?

Your grandchildren loved being around you; they all cherished you. Your children, grandchildren, great‑grandchildren, and especially your husband, Dadegbaja, miss you dearly. The entire family waits for your generous spirit, your readiness to ensure we are well, comforted, and our needs met.

Omillie, the gradual weakness and physical challenges that limited your ability to care for us and even for yourself gave us an indication that you were preparing to meet your Maker. Nevertheless, we eagerly anticipated your 85th birthday on 19th December 2025 and had already begun preparing for the celebration. Little did we know that death would lay its icy hand on you barely a month before.

Yet we believe that the Father needed you more. And though your departure has left us heartbroken, we take consolation in knowing that you are with your Maker in heaven.

Omillie, you were a true mother in every sense, and we were privileged to have you as ours. Your love and kindness nurtured us and touched everyone who crossed your path. You adopted and raised many children, a testament to your love for children, for God, and for His Word.

We lovingly called you Aunty Millie, Adjo, and Omillie, and you always responded with your amusing “Hello, dear mi,” which never failed to make us burst into laughter. Today, we stand here and call these names with the same affection yet there is no response, only a stark and painful silence.

We will never forget the Christian virtues you instilled in us. Your favorite hymn was MHB 634, and through it you taught us that no matter how tough life becomes, we must never let our anchor, Jesus Christ drift, but remain firmly rooted in Him. Your enthusiasm for the Women’s Fellowship (Yei Akpee), the Choir, and the Singing Band of Ebenezer Methodist Church inspired us to serve faithfully in the house of God.

You were our counsellor and our teacher. Your unwavering support and guidance have shaped us into who we are today. We are eternally grateful to God for the blessing of being your children.

With tears in our eyes, and with the painful realization that we shall never see you or hear your voice again on this side of life, we submit to God’s will for your journey.

You will be sorely missed, Omillie. We shall cherish your memories forever.

Well done, dear mother.

Till we meet again, we bid you farewell.

Rest in Perfect Peace, Ameley.
