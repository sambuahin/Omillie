“Jesus said to her, I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me will live, even though they die and whoever lives by believing in me will never die”. - John 11: 25-26

The demise of Sis. Millicent Buahin came to us as a surprise, especially because we had heard that the band had been invited by her to celebrate her birthday and were already making preparations toward that.

She was one of the founding members of the band, which was established on the 22nd of March, 2009. She was an Alto Leader and was very active at rehearsals as well as during Sunday church services until ill health and other challenges made it difficult for her to participate fully in Society and Circuit programmes.

Members of the band often visited her at home, singing some of the old songs to inspire and encourage her. She would joyfully join the choruses and always prayed that we would visit more often.

Auntie Millie, we know that you have journeyed into a better world, resting from the pains and sorrows of this life.

Rest well, Auntie Millie, till we meet again.
