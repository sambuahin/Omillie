Husband's Tribute

Read by: Mr. David Aaku

And I heard a voice from heaven saying, "Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on." "Yes," says the Spirit, "that they may rest from their labour, for their works follow them."

It is with the deepest sense of loss, yet with gratitude to God, that I pay this tribute to my dear wife Millicent, whose mortal remains lie before us.

It sometimes beats our imagination that even good people are swept away in the sleep of death; yet God’s ways are not our ways, nor are His thoughts our thoughts.

I met my dear wife at a function in Kotobabi years ago. We married and were blessed with three children: Kwabena Buahin Jnr, Providence Abena Abrefi Buahin, and Judith Akua Buahin.

All too soon, a strong yoke is broken. A mighty tree has fallen, leaving us all in sorrow, yet she has left her footprints in the sands of history. I take solace in the word of God, which says, "The Lord giveth, and the Lord taketh away; blessed be the name of the Lord." — Job 1:21

I will forever miss you and will never forget you. Your departure has created a deep vacuum. I was looking forward to your 85th birthday in December to honor your excellence.

God, in His own wisdom, has other plans for you and for all of us.

Until the sound of the last trumpet, when all souls shall rise and die no more, I say, "My dear wife Millie, rest until we meet again."
