In-laws' Tribute

“Jesus said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life. The one who believes in me will live, even though they die; John 11:25 NIV

Omillie, we were eagerly awaiting your birthday in December to honour and celebrate your life when unfortunately, God called you home.

You were indeed a mother whose care was woven into our little families. You were not just a mother-in-law but a true friend. You unreservedly shared very delicate stories of your life with us to teach us some lessons of life and we are immensely grateful. Your warmth and strength was always so admirable and it goes without saying that your cooking was superb. Your excitement whenever the family gathered for any meeting was always very refreshing. You always generously gave us kpekple and palm nut soup when it was Homowo yearly without fail until you became too weak to attend the festival and cook. You brought love and colour into the lives of many and selflessly cared for them. Thank you for all the lives you touched and a life well lived.

Thank you for being a good mother to us all.

Rest well, Omillie.
