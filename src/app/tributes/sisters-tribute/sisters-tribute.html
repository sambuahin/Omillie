Sisters' Tribute

Growing up, we were more than just sisters; we were a small, private world.

To lose our sister feels like losing part of ourselves. She was the one who knew our stories without us having to tell them, and the one who understood our silence.

Even though she is no longer here to walk beside us, we see her everywhere in the songs she loved, the advice she gave, and the kindness she showed others. She taught us how to be brave and how to love without conditions. We are heartbroken, but we are also profoundly grateful.

We will carry her light with us in every step we take, keeping our circle whole in our hearts until we meet again.
